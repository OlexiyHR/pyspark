## Dataset
We chose the [NYC taxi trip data](https://archive.org/details/nycTaxiTripData2013) dataset.

## Business questions
Below is a list of 20 business questions that we would like to answer when working on the project:

1. Which taxi drivers are the 10 most successful by the total amount of trips, total cost of their trips, and by tips received?
2. Which months / days of week are the most profitable in terms of total trip cost?
3. What are the traffic trends throughout the day? 
4. Which boroughs are the most popular as the pickup/dropoff location?
5. Which specific pickup or dropoff locations (i.e. airports, offices) have the highest volume of trips?
6. Which NYC borough has the biggest traffic?
7. Does the tip amount depend on the pickup / dropoff borough?
8. How does the tipping amount depend on the payment type?
9. How does the tipping amount depend on the distance / duration / time of the day?
10. Which rate codes are the most profitable?
11. Which vendor has the highest fare?
12. How much MTA tax is paid by taxi operators each month?
13. Does payment type depend on the total payment amount? (i.e. are people more inclined to pay with card when the price is higher?)
14. What is the distribution of the amount of passengers per trip?
15. Does the number of passengers affect the trip price?
16. How does the number of passengers depend on the time of day?
17. How does the number of passengers depend on the pickup location (borough)?
18. Which days or hours have the biggest amount of short trips (less than 3km)? 
19. What is the distribution of trip amounts by each vendor?
20. What is the average trip speed in each of the months?
